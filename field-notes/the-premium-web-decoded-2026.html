The formula — five laws

Numbered because it's a recipe
LAW 01

Typography is the brand

Two or three voices with strict jobs — or one typeface with total discipline (Rejouice runs Suisse Int'l alone). The logo barely matters; the type system is the identity.

Live — the three voices of this page
Grotesk does the work. MONO LABELS THE DATA · 2026-07-12 And the serif says the human thing.
LAW 02

Monochrome first, one signal colour

Off-white ground, near-black ink, exactly one saturated accent doing all the pointing. Colour arrives through photography and video — never through UI chrome.

LAW 03

Radius zero

Every premium site audited: border-radius 0. Sharp corners are the current "designed, not templated" tell. Rounded survives at the consumer-tool end; the craft tier is square.

LAW 04

The scale jump is violent

80px+ heroes against 14–16px body — a 5–6:1 jump with tight leading and negative tracking. Middle sizes are skipped on purpose; mono eyebrows stand in for the missing mid-weights.

Measured — undercurrents.show
84px over 14px body text. The gap is the hierarchy — nothing in between.

LAW 05

The grid shows, motion reveals

Hairline rules and visible structure; scroll does narrative work. The motion vocabulary: masked text rises, ↗ arrows as the universal affordance, hovers that displace rather than glow. Every section of this page enters exactly this way.

05

The verdict

The anti-template look is calcifying into a template. Laws one through five are the floor — every studio can copy them, and by next year every AI will. The signature is the one rule you break on purpose: Times New Roman in a Swiss layout, an ugly colour worn confidently, a system font set like it cost money.

STEP 1

Pick the school

The Greatest Websites canon — WebGL world, scroll story, editorial type, or product benchmark.

STEP 2

Dress it current

This shelf: three voices, monochrome + one signal, radius zero, violent scale, exposed grid.

STEP 3

Break one rule

Deliberately. That deviation is the signature — the part they remember.

STEP 4

Hold the gate

The Top-0.1% acceptance gate: mobile-first, reduced-motion, one flawless signature moment.

FULL DISCLOSURE: this page wears the uniform it describes — #F0F0F0 ground, #141414 ink, one #F3350C signal, three type voices, radius zero, masked reveals. That's the demonstration. The Times italic you keep noticing? That's law six.
