One studio. Every town on the route.

Most web studios treat "the Garden Route" as one generic region. We don't — George, Wilderness, Knysna, Mossel Bay, Sedgefield and Oudtshoorn are different towns with different guests, different competitors and different search habits, and we build for each one specifically.

The whole region is filling up at once.

George Airport — the gateway for the entire Garden Route — is getting a R310 million expansion from ACSA, doubling its capacity to 2 million passengers a year, with construction starting later this year. The Garden Route is already the Western Cape's second-fastest-growing region, and that growth doesn't stop at George's town line — it ripples down the N2 to every town along it.

A visitor or new resident researching "the Garden Route" hasn't picked a town yet. Whoever shows up in that early, broad search gets first look — before the visitor ever narrows it down to a specific town's guest houses or businesses.

Sources: George Herald, ACSA · Western Cape Government Socio-Economic Profile.
