The Garden Route is about to get a lot busier.

ACSA is investing R310 million to expand George Airport, doubling its capacity from around 900,000 to 2 million passengers a year. Construction starts later this year, with completion targeted for 2029.

Every OTA booking quietly costs you a cut, forever.

Booking.com and Airbnb typically take up to 15–18% commission off every booking they send you — on every guest, every stay, for as long as you rely on them. It's not a one-time fee. It's a permanent tax on your own rooms.

A website doesn't add a cost. It removes one, on every booking it brings in directly instead — and as more people fly into George and search for somewhere to stay before they've even landed, whoever has a website is the one they find.
